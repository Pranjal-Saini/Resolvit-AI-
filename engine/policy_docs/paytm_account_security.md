# Paytm account security: hacked or compromised account

Sources (paraphrased for retrieval, last checked 2026-11-15):
- Paytm Help, "Steps to recover your hacked Paytm account": https://paytm.com/support/using-the-paytm-app/steps-to-recover-your-hacked-paytm-account
- Paytm Blog, "Paytm fraud reporting timeline": https://paytm.com/blog/fraud-safety/paytm-fraud-reporting-timeline-expect-action
- Paytm Blog, "Step-by-step guide to filing a Paytm fraud complaint": https://paytm.com/blog/fraud-safety/step-by-step-guide-filing-paytm-fraud-complaint

## Compromised account
If a customer says their account is hacked, compromised, or shows transactions they did not make, they must report it immediately. The sooner a fraud is reported, the higher the chance that the bank can reverse the transaction or freeze the funds. The customer reports it in the Paytm app under 24x7 Help, choosing Account Security or Fraud/Scam, and includes the IDs of any unauthorized transactions. The Paytm helpline is 0120-4456-456.

## Blocking after a fraud report
Reporting unauthorized access leads to a precautionary block on the account and wallet so that no further money can leave it. This block protects the customer and is not permanent. It is lifted after the investigation and verification. If unauthorized bank transactions are seen, the customer should also contact their bank right away to block the debit or credit card, or to freeze the bank account temporarily.

## Securing the account
After a compromise the customer should change their password to a strong, unique one. They should open device management under Security & Privacy and log out of every unfamiliar or suspicious session. They should review Balance & History or Passbook and note any unauthorized transactions for the report. Fraudsters often make several small transactions, so the whole history should be checked.

## Never share OTP or PIN
Paytm will never ask for an OTP, password or UPI PIN. Customers should set an app lock with a PIN or biometrics. Messages that threaten to block the account unless KYC is completed are a common scam.

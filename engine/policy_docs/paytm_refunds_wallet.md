# Paytm refunds: source account and wallet

Sources (paraphrased for retrieval, last checked 2026-11-15):
- Paytm Help, "Payments and refunds on Paytm": https://paytm.com/support/payments/payments-and-refunds-on-paytm-key-things-to-know
- Paytm Help, "Paytm failed transaction refund not received": https://paytm.com/support/payments/paytm-failed-transaction-refund-not-received-know-this
- Paytm Payments, "Refund management": https://paytmpayments.com/docs/refund-management

## Refund to source
The refund for a failed transaction is credited back to the original source of payment, such as the bank account, credit card or UPI account used. Paytm starts the refund within minutes or hours of the failure, and it typically takes 3 to 7 bank working days to show in the source account.

## Wallet payments
If the customer paid from their Paytm Wallet, the refund is credited back to the Paytm Wallet, and it reflects immediately.

## Cancelled payments
When a successful payment is cancelled, for example a booking or an order, the refund follows the merchant's cancellation policy. Refunds for cancellations can take 5 to 15 working days to reflect after the cancellation. Eligibility for a cancellation refund depends on the merchant.

## Refund not received
If the refund has not arrived after the waiting period, the customer should raise a ticket through Help & Support in the Paytm app, quoting the Order ID or transaction ID. A bank statement showing the debit helps support trace the payment. The customer should not pay again for the same order while the refund is in progress.

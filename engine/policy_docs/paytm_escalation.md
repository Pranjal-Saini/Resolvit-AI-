## When to contact support
A customer should contact Paytm support when a failed transaction's refund has not arrived after 7 working days, when a cancellation refund has not arrived within the merchant's timeline, or immediately when there is fraud or unauthorized access. Support is reached through 24x7 Help in the Paytm app, or through the Send a Query to Customer Care form.

## Information support needs
The customer should give the transaction ID or Order ID, the amount, the date, and if possible a bank statement showing the debit. For fraud, they should list every unauthorized transaction ID at once.

## Ombudsman
If a complaint about a failed transaction is not redressed within the RBI turnaround time, or the customer is not satisfied with the reply, they can file a complaint under the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.
